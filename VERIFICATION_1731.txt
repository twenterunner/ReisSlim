STRICT TRIP-SPECIFIC IMAGE ACCEPTANCE

Hard requirement:
A proposal is displayed only when its image passes BOTH:
1. photographic-quality validation; and
2. specific destination relevance validation.

Rejected automatically:
- paper/documents/scans/brochures/flyers/posters/pages;
- maps/locator maps/flags/logos/diagrams/icons;
- signs/plaques/tickets/menus/postcards;
- drawings/illustrations/paintings/engravings;
- weak/small/extreme-aspect images;
- images whose metadata/title does not name the actual proposed region/destination.

The previous image cache is intentionally invalidated, so already cached weak
images such as the reported "piece of paper" cannot survive the update.

If no qualifying image exists, the proposal remains hidden and another candidate
must be used. There is no generic stock-photo fallback.
