Six common tasks. Open “Guided workflow” in the app and follow the same sequence.

Golden rule: Understand → Act → Evidence → Recheck / Replan → Continue. If an item is blocked, do not work around it by manually forcing dates or acknowledging the warning.

If recovering the target programme requires moving other programmes, it previews the programme-by-programme impact for Accept/Reject. Only hard physical/governance blockers require a Resolve action.
