ReisSlim v1.7.24 · Build 1724

Implemented:
- Proposal portfolio itself now starts with an intuitive visual comparison before the user selects trips for the dedicated compare feature.
- Three portfolio views: Tegels, Tabel and Kaart.
- Tegels is the default: compact two-column cards show score, weather, cost, distance, strongest match and weakest point.
- Tabel gives a dense at-a-glance comparison and highlights the winner for match, weather, cost and distance.
- Kaart plots all currently proposed destinations together and provides a compact proposal strip below it.
- Each compact proposal can be chosen, expanded to full details, or dismissed directly.
- The floating Vergelijk bar has been removed completely. It can no longer flash, intercept taps, or overflow the mobile viewport.
- The existing dedicated comparison section remains available after selecting proposals with their inline compare controls.
