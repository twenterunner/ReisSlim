ReisSlim v1.7.23 · Build 1723

Implemented:
- Continuous proposal review queue. Dismissing a proposal triggers repeated new live search rounds until the visible queue is refilled or multiple genuinely empty source rounds occur.
- No automatic stop at 6 or at 100 dismissals; 100 is a progress target, not a hard ceiling.
- Background prefetch keeps a reserve of unseen candidates.
- Review badge shows current cards and progress toward 100 reviewed options.
- Redundant summary paragraph below proposal pictograms is hidden.
- Comparison redesigned into three views:
  1. Overzicht: swipeable visual cards with score, cost, distance, weather, strengths and weakness.
  2. Scorekaart: selected criteria + core constraints, with winner highlighted on every row.
  3. Kaart: selected destinations plotted together from the departure point on an OpenStreetMap basemap.
- Added "Waarom A boven B?" delta summary.
